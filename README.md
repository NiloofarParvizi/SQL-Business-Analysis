# SQL Business Analytics Project

A SQL-based data analytics project focused on analyzing orders, customers, revenue, acquisition channels, and operational performance for an on-demand transportation and delivery platform.

## Project Overview

This project uses SQL to answer practical business questions and transform transactional data into meaningful metrics and customer insights.

The analysis covers:

* Order and revenue performance
* City and category analysis
* Fulfillment performance
* Customer retention and repeat purchases
* Acquisition channels and CAC
* Cohort analysis
* Marketing campaign assessment
* RFM customer segmentation
* Fraud detection
* Operational and courier demand analysis
* Discount analysis

## Key Analysis

### Order & Revenue

* Average Order Value (AOV) per city
* Number of orders per city
* Daily and weekly order volume
* Orders by day of week
* GMV, AOV, and total orders by city and category

### Fulfillment

* Overall fulfillment ratio
* Fulfillment ratio by city
* Fulfillment ratio by city and day of week

### Customer Analysis

* Repeat customer analysis
* Customers with at least 2 purchases
* Average time from registration to first purchase

### Acquisition & Marketing

* Acquisition channel analysis
* Paid CAC analysis
* Overall CAC and related business metrics
* Marketing campaign assessment

### Customer Segmentation

* Cohort analysis
* RFM segmentation based on:

  * Recency
  * Frequency
  * Monetary

### Operational Analysis

* Potential high-demand hours for courier planning
* Discount-to-sales analysis by city
* Fraud pattern detection

## Tools

* SQL
* PandaSQL

### Dataset

The dataset was provided as part of the  course and is not included in this repository.

## Key Findings

The analysis highlights differences in order volume, revenue, fulfillment performance, customer behavior, acquisition efficiency, and discount patterns across cities and time periods.

It also demonstrates how customer-level analysis such as **Cohort Analysis and RFM Segmentation** can provide a deeper understanding of retention and customer value, while operational analysis can support decisions around fulfillment and courier capacity.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to move beyond basic querying and address practical business problems through **data exploration, metric development, customer analytics, and business analysis**.
